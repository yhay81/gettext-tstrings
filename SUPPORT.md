# Support

Choose the channel that matches what you need:

- **Usage and setup:** ask in
  [Q&A Discussions](https://github.com/yhay81/gettext-tstrings/discussions/categories/q-a).
- **Ideas and design questions:** start an
  [Ideas Discussion](https://github.com/yhay81/gettext-tstrings/discussions/categories/ideas).
- **Reproducible defects:** open a
  [bug report](https://github.com/yhay81/gettext-tstrings/issues/new?template=bug.yml).
- **Security vulnerabilities:** follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and report
  privately.

The project covers the t-string ↔ gettext convention, its Python runtime, and
its Babel integration. General gettext, Babel, framework, and translation-tool
support is best handled by those projects unless the problem is specific to
`gettext-tstrings`.
